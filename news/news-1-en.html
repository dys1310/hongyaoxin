Showcasing at a Major Industry Exhibition, Connecting Global Resources

Shenzhen Hongyaoxin Garment Equipment participated in XX exhibition, achieving brand and business gains
Date: 2026-08-03 | Source: Hongyaoxin
Shenzhen Hongyaoxin Garment Equipment has been deeply engaged in R&D and production of intelligent garment equipment for many years, specializing in heat-transfer machines, automatic screen printing machines, and hanger spraying production lines. To expand domestic and overseas sales channels and enhance brand awareness in the sewing equipment industry, the company participated in the XX exhibition at XX Exhibition Center from XXXX to XXXX.

1. Major industry exhibition bringing the whole supply chain together

The XX exhibition gathered hundreds of high-quality equipment manufacturers across the domestic sewing supply chain, attracting garment factories, industrial parks, foreign trade companies, and regional agents.

2. Full-range equipment on display, in-person demos reduce purchasing concerns

Hongyaoxin displayed core product matrices covering popular industrial sewing and printing equipment, upgraded intelligent automation machines, and custom special machines. On-site demos allow buyers to verify workmanship, run trial operations, and evaluate automation and consumable compatibility.

3. Strong booth traffic, new and existing customer wins

Long-term partners visited the booth for deep discussions and order confirmations, while many new prospective customers exchanged business information and planned follow-up trials and quotations.

4. Brand momentum upgraded

The exhibition strengthened Hongyaoxin's brand image of reliable R&D, customization capacity, stable delivery, and nationwide after-sales service.

5. Post-show follow-up and long-term strategy

After the exhibition, sales and technical teams continue customer follow-ups, sample testing, contract confirmation and scheduling. Hongyaoxin will keep participating in major domestic and overseas trade shows to support channel expansion and overseas markets.
